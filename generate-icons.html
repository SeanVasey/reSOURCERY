reSOURCERY Icon Generator

Regenerates the PNG icon set from the opaque source resourcery-icon-ios.svg (the full-bleed app mark). This is the zero-dependency, browser-based fallback — a faithful SVG rasterizer such as resvg or CairoSVG is preferred for production. Click the button, then move the downloaded files into the paths shown. favicon.ico (multi-resolution 16/32/48) must be assembled from the favicon PNGs with a dedicated tool and is not produced here.
